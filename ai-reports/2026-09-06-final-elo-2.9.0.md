# FINAL analysis — forge 2.9.0 inside and out, ELO vs the field
Date: 2026-09-06 | sources: live web + own battery | v1 (capstone; prior
ai-reports stay as evidence, this one judges)

## 1. What forge IS, deepest level (one page)
Text transforms, never executed code, never a parser beyond tokenizing:
`nolog` (drop logs) → `strip` (drop comments) → `short` (rename single-decl
locals, property/pattern-safe) → `num` (ints to hex) → `simp` (!0, folding,
dead branches) → `crypt` (long strings to XOR-hex calls, split) → `pack`
(3 segments, per-file salt, rotation, FNV "signature"). Output is PURE DATA
(`FS:2`); a 1 KB runner (tag→gate→verify→concat→decode→Function) executes it.
Deterministic per seed, sub-second builds, single-digit-ms startup. Trust
rests on TLS + pinned URLs + private raw — never on the sig (public format).

## 5. Reading the table (where we bleed)
- **Control flow (1/12)** is two-thirds of the gap to j-obfuscator. We have
  none; they have flattening even at low presets. Deliberate (mobile perf),
  but it is THE gap.
- **Locks (0/8)**: no domain/date binding at all. Owner-declined for forge
  core (developer choice) — still a scoreboard zero until SOME layer has it.
- **Strings (8/14)**: static XOR, no rotate/shuffle/rc4, no wrappers. Salt
  (2.9.0) closed the worst of it; rotation is disk-level only.
- **Tamper (3/10)**: accident-sig + gate only. No self-check, no interval,
  no console-kill switch (we do it at build = cleaner, but runtime has zero).
- **Workflow (7/12)**: no CI, Windows out, single consumer, docs young.
  Cheapest points on the board.

## 6. Where we LEAD (defend these)
- Determinism 6/6 (seeded, diffable — j-obfuscator needs config for this).
- Data-file model (code never rests as code — nobody else in-league does it).
- Audit gate + loud aborts + battery culture (veil is the only rival with a
  stated threat model; ours is enforced by tests).
- Perf 10/10 and 1 KB runner (nothing touches this).

## 7. Path to 75+ (tie j-obfuscator LOW) without leaving the league
Already roadmapped (elo-roadmap): tripwire, --seed (DONE in 2.9.0: salt is
seed-determinism), simplify (DONE), splitStrings (DONE). Remaining cheap:
string rotate/shuffle in crypt (not just disk), runtime console-kill option,
CI + Windows path, QUICKSTART example pair. Control-flow stays out by design;
locks stay developer-side by decision. That ceiling is ~80 — honestly labeled.

## 8. Final verdict
forge 2.9.0: **58/100, 3rd in-league** behind javascript-obfuscator OSS (75)
and veil (61), ahead of JS-Confuser (55). Best-in-league at determinism,
startup cost, and workflow honesty; worst at control flow and locks — both
refused on principle, not missed by accident. For its actual job (protecting
small userscripts for a technical audience), it is the RIGHT tool in its
league's top half; for hostile analysts with budgets, nothing in this league
(including us) survives — that tier starts at Jscrambler money.
